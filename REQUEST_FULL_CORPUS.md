# Requesting the Full Corpus

The public `evil-ai-bench` repository intentionally omits executable prompts and raw per-scenario transcripts. The complete corpus is available to qualified researchers on request.

To request access, contact the corresponding author listed in the manuscript and include:

- Name, affiliation, and institutional email address.
- Research purpose and planned use of the benchmark.
- Confirmation that evaluation will be conducted only in authorized test environments.
- Agreement to the terms in [ACCEPTABLE_USE.md](ACCEPTABLE_USE.md).
- Whether raw result artifacts are also required for reproducibility.

Approved researchers receive the full JSON suites for `attacks/`, `benign/`, and `agentic-capability/`, plus any requested raw result artifacts needed to reproduce the published aggregate figures.
